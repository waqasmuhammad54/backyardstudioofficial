## Authority links and citation readiness

Time-sensitive claims link to primary sources, including the Dubai Film and TV Commission, Dubai Civil Aviation Authority, UAE General Civil Aviation Authority, Google Ads, Google Analytics and LinkedIn. Each guide includes:

- an answer-first summary;
- visible source cards and review date;
- Article, BreadcrumbList and ItemList structured data where applicable;
- stable headings and concise Q&A blocks for human readers and answer engines;
- a short reuse note allowing limited quotation with attribution and a canonical backlink.

FAQPage markup is deliberately omitted because the site is not eligible for Google's restricted FAQ rich-result treatment. The visible questions remain useful without misleading search markup.

## Link-earning and distribution plan

External publication must use a verified Backyard company or staff account. Do not buy links, mass-submit directories, copy the full guide to other sites or use automated comment links.

1. Publish an original LinkedIn summary for each guide from the company page or an authorised team member. Link to the full canonical guide and add a real production observation.
2. Create one short explanatory video for the permit guide and one for the drone checklist. Put the guide URL in the YouTube description and embed or link the video back from the relevant guide after publication.
3. Send the production-brief template to existing agency, event, venue and marketing partners who already brief video work. Ask them to link to the free template only when it genuinely helps their clients.
4. Offer the permit and drone guides as reference material to Dubai event, location and production communities. Ask for corrections or missing official-source updates before asking for a link.
5. Answer relevant Quora or professional-community questions in full. Link only when the Backyard guide supplies useful detail that does not fit naturally in the answer, and disclose the relationship.
6. Publish a shorter, original Medium or LinkedIn article rather than duplicating the complete guide. Point readers to the canonical Backyard resource for the calculator, template or source list.
7. Pitch UAE marketing and business publications with a data-led angle based on anonymised production planning patterns. Do not claim proprietary market statistics until the underlying sample and method are documented.
8. Review earned links monthly for relevance and referral quality. Keep editorial links; reject paid-link schemes and manipulative exchanges.

## Measurement

Track each new URL separately in Search Console and analytics. Review after enough impressions accumulate, not immediately after launch:

- indexed/canonical status;
- impressions, clicks, average position and query mix;
- engaged sessions and template downloads;
- brief submissions or qualified contact actions;
- referral visits and genuinely earned linking domains;
- citations in answer engines using manual source verification.

Use campaign-tagged URLs for authorised social and partner distribution. Record the source, publisher, publication date, destination URL and outcome so reach work stays auditable.

## Outreach status

No external post, email, community answer or link request was sent as part of the website release. Those actions require the appropriate verified company account and an authorised human identity. The on-site content and linkable assets are ready for that distribution work.
