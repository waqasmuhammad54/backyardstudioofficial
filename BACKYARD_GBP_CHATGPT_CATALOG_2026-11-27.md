## Approved business description

Backyard Studio Official is a full-service video and photography production company founded in Dubai in 2019. We create corporate and brand films, DVC and TV commercials, event coverage, wedding photography and videography, social media and Reels, podcasts, product and food content, fashion, hotel, real estate, automotive, documentary, testimonial, live-stream and drone production. Our team handles concept, scripting, locations, permits, crew, filming, editing, colour, sound and platform delivery. We serve clients across Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Al Ain, Sharjah, Ajman, Umm Al Quwain, Ras Al Khaimah and Fujairah, with production planned around each venue, audience and approval process.
